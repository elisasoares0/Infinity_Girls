                        What is mSv?

                        Millisievert (mSv) measures how much radiation your body absorbed. NASA limits each astronaut to 600 mSv over a whole career, the same limit for women and men.

                        What does this mean for fertility?

                        Reproductive cells are among the most radiation-sensitive in the body. Ovaries do not renew their egg cells, so damage adds up over a career. The cells that make sperm are also sensitive, and testosterone drops after long flights before recovering in about 15 days. A three-year Mars mission is estimated at about 400 mGy to the ovaries, below the levels linked to infertility, but heavy cosmic-ray particles are still poorly understood. Talk to your flight surgeon in a private conference if you have questions.
