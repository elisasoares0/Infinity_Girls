Reproductive health and radiation

Your dose so far
85 mSv
Projected at mission end
168 mSv

The cells that make sperm are among the most radiation-sensitive in the body. Studies of spaceflight found lower testosterone, less mobile sperm and more DNA damage in sperm. In astronauts, testosterone dropped on landing day and returned to normal in about 15 days.

Nothing is logged here. If you want a testosterone check or have questions about fertility, ask in your private medical conference.

Help research

How long missions affect male reproductive health still needs research, and studies so far are small.
