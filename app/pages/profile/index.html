    Voice assistant

    In an emergency it says your vital signs and what to do, step by step, and tells the crew where you are. It speaks with this device's own voice, works without connection, and every word also shows on screen.

    Where the crew finds you in an emergency. It follows your routine; change it when you move.

    This browser cannot speak: the instructions show on screen only.

    Reminders

    At the time of each health task, dose of medicine or exam, a message arrives in the health messages chat, with Done (or Taken), In 15 min and Open. The chat opens by itself for a new message, never takes over what you are doing, and folds into a small button when nothing waits; messages on screen always stay on.

    Device notifications: off.

    Nausea mode

    With nausea or vomiting, reading is hard. Nausea mode shows the few steps that help in large text on top of every page, makes the rest smaller and stops all motion. It turns on by itself when you log nausea or vomiting on your body, and off 24 hours after the last log.

    Sign out

    You go back to the sign-in screen. Your account, health data, privacy choices and tasks stay saved on this device.
