Design system

Astro Health design system

A calm mission control: precise, reliable and human. Material 3 gives the structure (tokens, roles, states), Apple's guidelines the finish (clarity, glass only in navigation), on the NASA Space Apps brand.

Principles

  1. Health first, chrome second. The readings are the content; navigation steps back.
  2. Never color alone. Every status has a color, an icon and a word.
  3. What, why, what to do now. Every alert says how to get back to normal and when to call the flight surgeon.
  4. Readable in any light. Text at least 4.5:1, graphics 3:1, in both themes; 48px touch targets.

Logo

Inline SVG with theme colors: Deep Blue orbit on light, Neon Yellow orbit on dark. Minimum symbol width 24px; never stretched, recolored, rotated or shadowed.

Brand rules

From the NASA Space Apps brand guidelines (pesquisas/brand.pdf). They hold on every screen, in both themes.

  • The palette leans on blue. Every text and background pair is at least 4.5:1 (checked in colors.css).
  • Neon Yellow is never a background. Only an accent for text, buttons and symbols, and sparingly: the accent button, the focus ring and the logo's orbit in dark.
  • Rocket Red is never a background. Only lines and charts. The critical color for fills is Martian Red.
  • Running text sits only on Deep Blue, Electric Blue or white. Team decision: the dark theme uses neutral black and dark gray backgrounds (#121212 page, #1C1C1E cards), like most sites, and keeps the brand blues for the details: links and buttons, tinted badges, the selected tab, the Deep Blue footer, the hero gradient and the space band.
  • One gradient: linear, Electric Blue at the top left to Deep Blue at the bottom right, at 45°, smooth (the page heros).
  • Headings in Fira Sans Black or Bold, body text in Overpass Regular, Bold and Italic.
  • The standard logo first; the Motif and Small versions only where space is tight.
  • A symbol only when its meaning is unmistakable; otherwise a text label. Icons come from Phosphor.
  • Patterns, if used, at about 25% over a brand blue, never behind running text.
  • Three decorations only (decoration, not information; hidden in high contrast mode): a wave on top of the footer, a 4px gradient line in the brand colors at the end of each hero, and the curve of a planet's horizon on top and bottom of the astronaut band.

Motion and Lottie

Short, calm and optional. Lottie animations drawn for the app (tools/motion.js), played by lottie-web (MIT) only when needed.

  • success-checkA task ticked
  • heart-pulseAll of today's goals met
  • orbit-loaderWaiting for the health server
  • sync-doneRecords synced
App.motion.play("success-check", host)           // once
App.motion.play("orbit-loader", host, { loop: true })
App.motion.stop(host)
  • Decoration only: the host is aria-hidden and the same news is always in text next to it.
  • The player and the file load on the first play, never with the page.
  • Reduced motion (device setting) and nausea mode: the last frame, still.
  • Never in the SOS, an emergency guide or a critical alert.
  • One color, the text color where it plays, so it follows the theme.
  • Transitions use --motion-* and --ease-* (100 to 450 ms); only transform and opacity move.

Hierarchy and spacing

1. Title
Card title · --text-lg (18px) 700
The question the chart answers
2. Key data
Summary · --text-base (16px) regular
The answer in one sentence, with the number
3. Value labels
On the marks · 12px 700

4. Axes and legend
12px · secondary color
The lowest level; axes recessive, labels horizontal
  • Spacing: 8px between the summary, the legend and the chart (one group); margins inside the chart on the 8px grid; labels 8px from their mark.
  • Legend: above the chart on phones, so it stays in view while a finger is on the chart; below it from 768px.
  • Bold only on the title and the badge. Color for the data, neutrals for the rest; never color alone (the badge, the summary and the table say it in words).
  • Bars start at zero; few axis labels, never rotated; the normal range and limits are named in the legend.
  • Google Charts is not used: its terms do not allow hosting it or using it offline, and the app works without a connection in flight. Its gallery is a reference for chart forms only.
