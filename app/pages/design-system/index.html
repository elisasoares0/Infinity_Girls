Design system

Astro Health design system

A calm mission control: precise, reliable and human. Material 3 gives the structure (tokens, roles, states), Apple's guidelines the finish (clarity, glass only in navigation), on the NASA Space Apps brand.

Principles

  1. Health first, chrome second. The readings are the content; navigation steps back.
  2. Never color alone. Every status has a color, an icon and a word.
  3. What, why, what to do now. Every alert says how to get back to normal and when to call the flight surgeon.
  4. Readable in any light. Text at least 4.5:1, graphics 3:1, in both themes; 48px touch targets.

Logo

Inline SVG with theme colors: Deep Blue orbit on light, Neon Yellow orbit on dark. Minimum symbol width 24px; never stretched, recolored, rotated or shadowed.

Symbol and wordmark

Astro Health

The logo as a motif

  • The orbit becomes the loading spinner and the progress rings.
  • The ECG pulse is the trend line of every vital sign.
  • The satellite is the focus ring: Neon Yellow on dark, Neon Blue on light.

Color

The NASA Space Apps palette, used 60-30-10: base, blues, and at most 10% Neon Yellow (never on a light background). Ratios checked against WCAG 2.2.

Alerts by severity

Measurement saved

Info: a short confirmation, with Undo when possible.

Heart rate above your range

Attention: what happened, why, and what to do now. Back to normal when the next reading is in range.

Oxygen saturation 88%

Critical: a dialog that does not close by itself, numbered steps and "Contact flight surgeon".
