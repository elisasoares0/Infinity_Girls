About

About & Credits

Astro Health helps astronauts on long missions follow their own health: clear status colors, trends over time, plain-language advice and when to call the flight surgeon. It supports self-assessment and does not replace the flight surgeon.

The project

Built by team Infinity Girls for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. A prototype with simulated health data: not a medical device, and not endorsed by NASA.

Our logo

A heart for health and care, an ECG pulse for monitoring, and an orbit with a satellite for the mission. The satellite is also the app's focus ring.

NASA Space Apps Challenge

The colors, fonts and patterns follow the NASA Space Apps 2026 brand guidelines.

Use of AI

Parts of the code and the text were written with the help of Claude, an AI assistant by Anthropic, and reviewed by the team.

Inside the app, no AI decides what to do in an emergency: the voice and the guides follow fixed scenarios with the measured values.
