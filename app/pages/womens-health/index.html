    Contraception, blood clots and bones

    The pill roughly doubles the chance of a blood clot, but a NASA review found astronauts who use it have no higher risk than other women on the same pill. The three-month injection is usually avoided in flight because it adds bone loss to the bone loss space already causes.

    Urinary symptoms

    Urinary infections are a common reason women are sent home early. Logging early helps treat them before they get worse.

    Blood clots

    In space, blood shifts toward the head and clots have been found in astronauts' neck veins. Estrogen in the pill also raises the risk. Log any warning sign: it is optional and takes a few seconds.

    Return to gravity

    Women faint more often when standing up after flight, because they lose more blood plasma in space. Prepare in the last days before landing.

    1. Drink about 1 L of water with salt tablets in the hours before landing.
    2. Wear your compression garment for landing.
    3. Sit up first, then stand slowly with someone beside you on the first day.
    4. Follow the 45-day rehab plan: assisted walking, then water walking and cycling, then running and weights.

    The recovery team will meet you at landing.
